02 / The solution
A quality inspector that never loses focus

See the defect. Flag the operator. Protect the roll.

TexVision AI is a modular edge-computer-vision system that retrofits onto circular knitting and stenter machines. It detects defects in real time, at full machine speed, without waiting for the internet.

03 / Technology
A practical Industry 4.0 stack

Designed to fit the machines Bangladesh already has.

No replacement of capital equipment. TexVision adds a sensing, inference and reporting layer to an existing inspection point.

01

Sensing

Line-scan or industrial camera and LED ring lighting capture the fabric surface continuously.

02

Edge inference

On-device model classifies yarn breaks, holes, oil spots, shade variation and misalignment.

03

Control interface

Tablet and signal-light integration notify the floor team at the exact inspection point.

04

Analytics layer

Lightweight sync aggregates logs by line, shift and operator for compliance and waste analysis.
